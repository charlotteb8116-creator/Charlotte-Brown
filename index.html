The Diagnostic

Why Most Book Promotions Bleed Money Without Producing Royalties

Retail storefronts like Amazon are mathematical discovery engines, not libraries. Authors consistently hit commercial roadblocks by relying on antiquated promotional methods.

01

The Social Media Mirage

Posting on social media rarely converts casual scrollers into paying readers. Platform algorithms are designed to trap users within their own ecosystems, not send qualified buyer traffic to Amazon product pages.

02

The Promo Blast Trap

Cheap discount newsletter blasts trigger temporary, artificial download spikes. These spikes disrupt Amazon’s recommendation engine (COSMO/A9), degrading your long-term organic "Also Bought" associations.

03

Uncalibrated Ad Spend

Pouring money into Amazon Ads or Meta Ads without addressing underlying retail conversion bottlenecks simply burns capital faster. Traffic must arrive at a mathematically optimized sales page.
